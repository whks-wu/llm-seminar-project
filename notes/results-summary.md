Setting `pad_token_id` to `eos_token_id`:None for open-end generation.

注意结尾是 None ——意思是这个模型的 eos_token_id 是空的。

这说明 GPT-BERT 没有结束符。 它没有任何办法表示"我说完了"，所以每次生成都必然跑满 max_new_tokens 才停。昨天那个 The answer is that the answer is that... 循环，一部分原因就在这里——它想停也停不了。

对 pilot 的直接影响：base LM 的输出必须你自己截断，不能指望模型自己收尾。这就是之前说的「few-shot 用换行分隔、在第一个换行处切」的实际必要性——那是唯一的停止机制。

Qwen 有正常的 eos，会自己停，所以不受影响。

Qwen-3B:
"The sentence is almost correct, but it can be improved for clarity and grammatical correctness. Here's a revised version: ..." The model is performing **“improved writing”**, not just “correcting grammatical errors.” It even says itself that the sentence is “almost correct, but could be expressed more clearly.”

This directly leads to overcorrection—and the reason is task understanding, not the model's size. If your prompt simply says “correct the grammatical errors,” the model will still go ahead and rewrite the text for better fluency.
